Welcome to TravelToLight

We sincerely thank you for your association with us over all these years. From the very beginning, our intention was to support honest and dedicated yatris in reaching this blessed destination—especially at a time (around 2007) when very little information was available on the internet.

Over the years, things have changed, and a wealth of information is now easily accessible through the internet and social media. In light of this, we have decided to move on, realizing that our role in providing such information is no longer essential.

We hope that only sincere yatris and trekkers continue to visit this sacred place with the true intention of seeking the blessings of Lord Shiva, while also respecting and preserving nature.


Thank you!
